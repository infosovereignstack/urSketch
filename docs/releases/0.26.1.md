# urSketch 0.26.1

Remove the room invitation controls, hidden invite-code parser and automatic
joining from invite URLs. Players search a host ship and select a room, or
reconnect to their most recent room. Opening /play still does not search or
join automatically.

Move the first-game Start action into the yellow banner at the top of the
canvas. Only the owner sees it, and at least two players must be present. The
artist's word/rules prompt uses that same banner during drawing. Subsequent
games continue starting automatically.

Multiword hints separate words with blank space instead of a slash. Letter
spacing, revealed letters, hint limits and scoring remain unchanged.

# urSketch 0.26.2

The supplied screen recording contains a roughly 60-millisecond fragment of
Fireworks.mp3 repeating during the late winner screen. The waveform comparison
is recorded in `artifacts/fireworks-noise-analysis.json`. It is distinct from
the four-Hz countdown tick. The recording alone cannot identify the exact
browser/OS failure; Safari has a documented related issue involving stale
output on buffer underrun ([WebKit release notes](https://webkit.org/blog/18194/release-notes-for-safari-technology-preview-251/)).

Fireworks now use a single finite 20-second PCM track assembled from the supplied
MP3 at decode time. No native game-audio source loops. The track retains the
50% gain, includes a short fade to silence, and has an audio-clock stop plus
wall/monotonic-clock expiration. Completed finale identities remain finished
through repeated room updates and early-ended callbacks. Interrupted playback
resumes at the current offset inside the original audio window.

The audio destination suspends during the silent remainder of the finale,
including after an overlapping room cue finishes. New-round and turn-to-draw
cues wake it again. The 40-second visuals, countdown cadence, game rules and
supplied media files are unchanged.

Chromium and WebKit audio tests cover actual decoded PCM, full real-time
20-second playback with frequent room updates, silent output suspension,
new-game wakeup, mute/unmute, interruption/resume, clock divergence and late
updates. The full game-interface preview also passed. An affected physical
iPad/iPhone still needs confirmation after refreshing to this release.

Installed/published comet checks also passed, including exact equality of the
served audio source with the tested file and the public 0.26.2 Treaty version.

    WELCOME TO URSKETCH

    A picture is worth… points.

    Each player takes a turn drawing. Your word is assigned automatically. Illustrate it with the drawing tools. No letters, numbers, or giving the answer away!

    Everyone else has 120 seconds to guess. The first correct guess starts a 20-second finish timer. A round ends immediately if all guessers solve it. Correct guesses earn 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, then 5 points each. The drawer earns 10 points for the first correct guess and 1 for each additional guess. Give hint can be used three times before anyone guesses correctly: first it shows the letter count, then reveals one random letter per press. The artist’s first-solve reward becomes 5, 4, then 3 points. Additional correct guessers still add 1 point each. No correct guesses means no drawer points. Each game has 10 drawing rounds, followed by a 40-second winner celebration. The next game starts automatically. Rooms hold at most 10 players.

    Search a friend's host ship or share your room's invite. Every player joins through their own Urbit ship with urSketch installed.

    Drawing tools and shortcuts

    Pen P · Brush B · Spray Y · Eraser E · Rectangle S · Circle C · Line L · Zoom Z. Repeat S or C to toggle solid / outline shapes.

    + / − zoom · Space pans · Space+Alt zooms in · Space+Command zooms out · Alt picks a color · D toggles black / white.

    ↑ / ↓ change brightness · ← / → change width · 1–8 set width · 9 sets 16px · 0 sets 32px · Tab switches Game / Chat. Shift+Clear fills with your color. Shift+Zoom zooms out; double-click Zoom to reset zoom and pan.

    Drag palette grips to rearrange them outside the canvas; double-click a grip to rotate. Your palette arrangement is saved.

How to play

Choose Play now to create a room or search a friend's ship. Each player plays through their own ship.

Take turns choosing a secret word and drawing it in 80 seconds. Guess the artist's word in Game; use Chat to talk. No letters or numbers in the drawing.

Correct guesses earn 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, then 5 points each. The artist earns 10 points for the first correct guess, plus one for each additional guesser.

Give hint can be used three times before the first correct guess. It shows the letter count, then reveals one random letter per press. The artist's initial reward becomes 5, 4, then 3 points. With no correct guesses, the artist earns no points.

Drawing shortcuts: Pen P, Brush B, Spray Y, Eraser E, Rectangle S, Circle C, Line L, Zoom Z. +/− zoom; double-click Zoom to reset; Space pans.
